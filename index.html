Hajj & Umrah

As part of its commitment to employee well-being, ORION facilitates the opportunity to perform the Holy Hajj and Umrah each year, for a selected group of employees. The company undertakes all related formalities and bears the full cost of airfare, accommodation, and other associated expenses.

Winter Relief Distribution

Each year, as part of its Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) commitment, ORION distributes winter related essentials like blankets, sweaters, shawls and timely support across cold prone areas of Bangladesh. Specially, during the heavy cold waves a dedicated team of ORION works for the vulnerable communities with compassion.

Iftar Distribution during Ramadan

Ramadan is regarded as a sacred month that nurtures compassion, generosity, and the spirit of giving. Guided by these values, ORION organizes Iftar distribution activities during the whole month of Ramadan to support the poor and slum dwellers. With the purpose of complete support to the underprivileged members of society, additional dinner packets are provided with the nutritious Iftar on a daily basis. This initiative reflects ORION’s ongoing commitment to supporting the less fortunate and spreading the joy of Ramadan among communities in need. The company remains dedicated to continuing this noble endeavor in the years ahead.
